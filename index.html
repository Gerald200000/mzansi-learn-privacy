Mzansi Learn – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Mzansi Learn (“the app”) is a free study app for South African high-school learners in Grades 10 to 12, developed by Pureform Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you use the app. We wrote it with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind.

The short version

  • You don’t need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or school.
  • Your answers, scores, streak and settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. We only ask for non-personalised, teen-appropriate ads.
  • We don’t sell or share any personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • your grade, language, daily goal and exam date;
  • which questions you answered, the marks you gave yourself and your bookmarks;
  • your mock exam results, study streak and daily quiz;
  • how many free memos and mock exams you have used today, and which memos you opened in the last 7 days.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete your study progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress, and delete everything by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch to unlock extra memos or mock exams. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash logs.

We set every ad request to non-personalised (ads are not based on your interests or browsing history) and limit ads to teen-appropriate content. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Checking today’s date

The app gives a number of free memos and mock exams each day. To stop the phone’s date being changed to get more, the app may ask a Google web address for the current date and time when you are online. This request contains no information about you or your progress, but, like any internet connection, it shows Google your IP address. The app works offline and simply skips this check when there is no connection.

4. Sharing the app and rating it

If you tap Share Mzansi Learn, your phone’s own share menu opens with a short message and a link to the app. You choose who to share with; the app does not see your contacts or messages. If you tap Rate the app, the Google Play Store opens. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy.

5. Children and learners

The app is meant for learners aged 13 and over (Grades 10–12) and is not directed at children under 13. POPIA treats anyone under 18 as a child. For that reason we don’t collect personal information ourselves, and we only allow non-personalised, teen-appropriate ads. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Information on your phone is protected by your phone’s own security, such as your screen lock. Google’s ad software and the date check use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because your study information stays on your phone, you can delete it yourself at any time. For information held by Google, see Google’s privacy policy. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.
